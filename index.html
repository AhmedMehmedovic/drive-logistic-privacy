DriveLogistic Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DriveLogistic ("the App") accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when it is used by authorized drivers, employees, and logistics personnel.

DriveLogistic is provided by Herceg d.o.o., located in 75350 Srebrenik, Bosnia and Herzegovina ("Herceg", "we", "us", or "our").

DriveLogistic is a business application. User accounts are created and managed by the employer or organization that provides access to the App. The App does not provide public account registration.

Depending on the contractual relationship, the organization that provides a user account may determine how business and operational information is processed, while Herceg d.o.o. may process that information on the organization's behalf.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Account and personal information

We process account information required to authenticate users and provide access to authorized features. This information may include:

  • username and internal user ID;
  • authentication credentials and access tokens;
  • display name and account profile information;
  • email address, phone number, or business address, if provided by the organization;
  • assigned roles, permissions, team, and organizational position.

Authentication tokens and session information are stored using protected device storage where supported by the operating system.

2.2 Location information

With the user's permission, DriveLogistic collects approximate and precise device location information.

Background location: Location may be collected while the App is open and while it is running in the background, including when the App is closed or not actively in use.

Location information is used to:

  • display the driver's current position;
  • support delivery and logistics coordination;
  • show relevant deliveries and pallets on a map;
  • associate deliveries and pallets with their current location;
  • provide navigation and directions;
  • allow authorized logistics personnel to monitor delivery progress.

Users can decline or revoke location permission through their device settings. Location-dependent features may not function correctly after permission is declined or revoked.

2.3 Photos and signatures

Users may voluntarily capture or upload photos as delivery, collection, pallet, or logistics documentation. The App may also collect recipient signatures used as proof of delivery.

Photos and signatures are associated with the relevant delivery, pallet, order, or logistics document and may be viewed by authorized personnel within the organization.

2.4 Operational information and App activity

The App processes operational information such as:

  • delivery and pallet status changes;
  • delivery notes and other user-provided content;
  • delivery, pallet, order, and customer references;
  • search queries, filters, and sorting preferences;
  • actions performed while completing assigned logistics tasks;
  • dates, times, and other information related to delivery activity.

2.5 Technical and diagnostic information

To maintain the App, identify errors, and improve reliability, we may process technical information including:

  • device manufacturer, brand, model, and device type;
  • operating-system name and version;
  • App version and build number;
  • crash logs, error messages, and stack traces;
  • network and API errors;
  • technical request context;
  • the internal user ID associated with an error report.

2.6 Google Maps information

DriveLogistic uses Google Maps services to provide map functionality. Google Maps SDK may automatically process request metadata, IP address, device metadata, crash information, a pseudonymous Maps SDK identifier, and map interaction events such as panning and zooming.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy .

3. How We Use Information

We use information processed through DriveLogistic to:

  • authenticate users and manage authorized access;
  • provide delivery and pallet management functionality;
  • coordinate drivers and logistics operations;
  • display maps, locations, and directions;
  • track delivery and pallet progress;
  • document and verify completed deliveries;
  • maintain security and prevent unauthorized access;
  • diagnose errors and improve App reliability;
  • comply with contractual and legal obligations.

We do not use personal information for third-party advertising or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

4. How Information Is Shared

Information may be made available to:

  • the employer or organization that created and manages the user's account;
  • authorized dispatchers, administrators, drivers, and logistics personnel;
  • hosting, infrastructure, logging, and technical service providers processing information on our behalf;
  • Google Maps services for map functionality and related diagnostics;
  • public authorities when disclosure is required by applicable law.

Photos, signatures, location records, and operational information are not shared with advertising companies.

The App may allow a user to manually share a photo through another application installed on the device. This sharing is initiated and controlled by the user and is subject to the privacy practices of the selected application.

5. Data Retention

Account information is retained while the associated business account is active and for any additional period required by the organization, contractual obligations, or applicable law.

Delivery records, status history, photos, signatures, notes, and location records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support logistics operations and meet the organization's contractual, operational, and legal requirements.

Diagnostic and crash information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate technical issues, maintain security, and improve App reliability.

When information is no longer required, it is deleted, anonymized, or securely archived where continued retention is legally required.

6. Data Management

DriveLogistic does not currently provide a user-facing feature or a direct mechanism for requesting deletion of personal data.

User accounts and operational data are created and managed by the employer or organization that provides access to DriveLogistic. Users should contact their organization's administrator regarding account information, corrections, or account management.

Information is retained and removed according to the contractual, operational, and legal requirements applicable to Herceg d.o.o. and the organization using DriveLogistic.

7. Data Security

We use administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal and sensitive information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data transmitted between the App and production servers is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption. Access to operational information is limited to authenticated and authorized users.

No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security, but we regularly review our safeguards and access controls.

8. International Data Processing

Some technical service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country. Where required, appropriate contractual, technical, and legal safeguards are applied.

9. Children's Privacy

DriveLogistic is a business application intended for authorized professional users. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Hosting of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be hosted using GitHub Pages. When a user visits this page, GitHub may process technical information such as an IP address and access logs for security and service operation.

For more information, review the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, applicable legal requirements, or our data-processing practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
